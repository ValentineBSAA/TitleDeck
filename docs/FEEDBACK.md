# Share your TitleDeck setup 💬

TitleDeck is shaped by the people who use it. **You do not need a bug report to join in.** Working features, small improvements, and favorite customizations are all worth hearing about.

## Show what you've made

Head to **[TitleDeck GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ValentineBSAA/TitleDeck/discussions)** and choose **New discussion** (you may need to sign in to GitHub). Pick the most suitable available category and tell us whatever you'd like:

- **A screenshot:** your customized Steam Home, Full Library Manager, or typography settings.
- **A tiny success story:** which title or description you personalized and whether it worked on your Deck.
- **Your favorite feature:** something you use regularly, or one thing you'd improve.
- **A question:** anything that was hard to understand when setting up TitleDeck.

**No formal template needed.** Even a sentence like “Renamed my non-Steam game and the new title stayed after a restart” helps the developer understand real-world use.

Before sharing screenshots, crop or cover account names, friends, notifications, private library entries, tokens, and other personal details. You never need to provide Steam credentials.

## Found a problem?

For reproducible bugs or focused new-feature requests, use the **[GitHub issue forms](https://github.com/ValentineBSAA/TitleDeck/issues/new/choose)** instead. Check the [known limitations](COMPATIBILITY.md) first, especially Game Info right-stick scrolling and read-only Art Hero positioning.

Useful bug reports include your TitleDeck, Decky Loader, and SteamOS versions; whether you use CSS Loader / Art Hero; controller type; and the steps to reproduce. [Troubleshooting](../SUPPORT.md) has more detail.
