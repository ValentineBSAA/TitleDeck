# TitleDeck: Real Steam Deck screenshots

**Seven screenshots from a physical Steam Deck running Gaming Mode**, captured during the **0.8.1** development cycle. These are intentionally retained as previews for **0.9.0 RC1** until new release-candidate screenshots exist.

> **Warning:** These photos may include a CSS Loader theme and do **not** verify ROG Ally, another SteamOS-like handheld PC, Steam Desktop Mode, Windows, Android or DroidDeck. Those environments are **untested and may not work correctly**.
